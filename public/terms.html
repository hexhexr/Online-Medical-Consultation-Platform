Terms of use

Plain-language terms for using the MedConsult platform and its AI-assisted routing feature.

Last updated: September 18, 2026

Purpose of MedConsult

MedConsult provides an online workflow for finding verified doctors, requesting appointments, exchanging consultation messages and receiving consultation-related records.

Medical limitation

The AI triage feature recommends a medical specialty from symptom text. It is not a medical diagnosis and should not be treated as one. Users should seek appropriate professional or emergency care when required.

Accounts

Users are responsible for maintaining the confidentiality of their account credentials. Doctor accounts are subject to administrator verification. Access may be suspended by an administrator where appropriate for platform operation.

Appointments

An appointment request is not confirmed until the doctor accepts it. Patients can cancel pending or confirmed appointments from their portal. Meeting links may be provided for confirmed consultations. Availability is determined by participating doctors.

Records

Prescriptions and invoices are created by doctors for completed consultations. Users should verify important medical instructions directly with the treating professional.

Production deployment

An organization deploying MedConsult publicly should replace or extend these project terms with legal terms appropriate to its services, jurisdiction, payment model, clinical responsibilities and data-processing arrangements.
