Privacy

Privacy notice

This page explains the information handled by MedConsult and the responsibilities that should be finalized before a real public deployment.

Last updated: September 18, 2026

Information handled by the platform

MedConsult may process account details such as name, email address, role, doctor specialty and biography. During use of the consultation workflow it may also process symptom descriptions, appointment information, consultation messages, prescriptions, invoices and administrative audit activity.

Why the information is used

The information is used to authenticate users, provide role-specific dashboards, recommend a medical specialty through the triage feature, manage appointment requests, support consultation communication, provide consultation records and help administrators operate the platform.

AI-assisted triage

Symptom text submitted to the triage feature is processed to recommend a specialty. The feature is not intended to produce a medical diagnosis. A production deployment should define retention rules for triage inputs and outputs based on the organization’s legal and operational requirements.

Access

Platform access is role-based. Patients, doctors and administrators receive different capabilities. Doctor accounts require verification before appearing as active providers.

Data protection responsibilities

The current project provides application-level account and role controls. Before a healthcare organization deploys the system publicly, it should complete a formal security, privacy, hosting, backup, retention, incident-response and regulatory review for the jurisdictions in which it operates.

Contact

Deployment-specific privacy contact details should be configured by the organization operating MedConsult.
