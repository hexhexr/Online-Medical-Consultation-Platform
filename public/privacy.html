Privacy Notice

How MedConsult collects, safeguards, and protects your personal health information and account data.

Last updated: September 18, 2026

Information Collected

MedConsult processes essential account details such as your name, email address, and role. When using the consultation services, we securely process symptom descriptions, appointment details, private consultation messages, digital prescriptions, and invoices.

How Your Information is Used

Your information is used solely to authenticate your account, provide personalized healthcare dashboards, suggest appropriate medical specialties through AI triage, manage appointment bookings, and facilitate secure communication with your attending physician.

Confidentiality of AI Symptom Triage

Symptom descriptions submitted to the symptom assistant are processed strictly to recommend an appropriate medical specialty. They are never shared publicly or used for commercial advertising.

Access & Confidentiality

Access to your medical records and consultation history is strictly restricted to you and your attending physician. We do not sell or rent patient health data to third parties.

Data Security Standards

We implement industry-standard encryption, secure session tokens, and strict access controls to safeguard your health records, prescriptions, and communication logs against unauthorized access.

Contact Privacy Support

If you have any questions or concerns regarding your health data or privacy rights, please reach out to our team via the Contact page.
