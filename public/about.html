About MedConsult

A clearer online consultation workflow

MedConsult brings patient guidance, verified doctor discovery, appointment handling, consultation messaging, prescriptions and invoices into one understandable platform.

Our purpose

Patients often have to move between disconnected channels to decide which doctor to contact, arrange a consultation, exchange information and keep records. MedConsult is designed to make those steps part of one continuous experience.

The system supports patients, doctors and administrators with role-specific workflows while keeping the user interface consistent across every area.

How the AI fits in

The triage feature takes symptom text and uses a trained machine-learning model to recommend a medical specialty. It helps route a patient to an appropriate type of doctor.

It is not a diagnostic system. The final medical assessment remains the responsibility of a qualified healthcare professional.

Three user roles

One platform, focused experiences

Patients

Find doctors, use specialty guidance, request appointments, communicate during confirmed consultations, and review records.

Doctors

Review requests, confirm consultations, communicate with patients, add meeting links, and create post-consultation records.

Administrators

Verify doctors, manage account status, review users, and inspect important system activity through audit logs.
